It hunts one repeatable moment

Markets overshoot obvious levels, run everyone's exits, then snap back. The bot waits for exactly that — then lets winners run and cuts losers instantly.

Big institutions cause the overshoot (they need everyone's orders to fill their size); small traders get caught by it. The Mind trades the snap-back with a tiny fixed risk, banks a third at its first target, and lets the rest ride as far as the day goes. A small edge, repeated ~427 times a year, compounding.
